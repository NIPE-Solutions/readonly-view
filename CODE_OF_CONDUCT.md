# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, accountability, and respect. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, threats, or publishing private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository maintainers using GitHub's private channels. Maintainers will investigate fairly, respect privacy, and may remove contributions or temporarily or permanently ban participation according to impact.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
